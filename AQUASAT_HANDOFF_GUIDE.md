## 1. Project goal in plain language

AquaSat is studying whether satellite images can estimate water storage in five Chennai-area reservoirs:

- Poondi
- Cholavaram
- Puzhal (Redhills)
- Chembarambakkam
- Veeranam

The main satellite measurement question is:

> Can an image-derived water area be calibrated against a same-date official CMWSSB storage reading to produce a useful storage estimate?

The forecast question is separate:

> Can recent official storage history, optionally supplemented by a previous calibrated satellite estimate, improve a 15-day forecast of future official storage?

The dashboard reads saved CSV outputs. The live 15-day forecast is generated by XGBoost using official storage history and a prior calibrated satellite estimate when available; that satellite history prefers Sentinel-2 and uses Sentinel-1 only to fill a missing same-lake/date S2 estimate.

## 4. Where NDWI, MNDWI, Sentinel-1, and XGBoost fit

### Image-to-water-area step

- **Sentinel-2 is optical imagery.** It measures reflected sunlight. NDWI/MNDWI are calculated from optical bands to classify candidate water pixels and compute area within a lake boundary.
- The active S2 water-area rules are **MNDWI > 0.10 for Poondi and Cholavaram, MNDWI > −0.10 for Puzhal and Chembarambakkam, and MNDWI > 0.00 for Veeranam**. The selected thresholds come from early-date comparison; the later 20% holdout is kept separate for reporting.
- Clear95 still means at least 95% clear optical coverage; it is separate from the MNDWI water threshold.
- **Sentinel-1 is radar.** Its water-area candidates come from low VV/VH backscatter threshold rules. There is no NDWI or MNDWI step in the S1 path.

### Water-area-to-storage step

`build_sentinel2_threshold_storage.py` maps the active selected water area to mcft against official CMWSSB readings for the same lake and exact date. For a particular image date, the calibration is fitted only on earlier matched official readings. Conflicted official pairs are excluded. This creates the active out-of-sample (OOS) satellite storage estimate. `build_sentinel2_storage.py` preserves the original-area comparison path.

### Forecasting step

- **Forecast target:** future official CMWSSB storage in mcft.
- **XGBoost baseline:** features from prior official storage history, recent changes/statistics, and calendar timing.
- **Live XGBoost with satellite feature:** the same official-history features plus the latest calibrated satellite storage estimate available at the forecast origin. The combined history prefers S2 and fills a same-lake/date S2 gap with S1. The model does not train directly on water-area hectares, MNDWI, VV, or VH pixels.
- **Prophet experiment:** uses official storage history to predict official storage. It is a comparison method, not the current dashboard generator.
- `chennai_lakes_combined.csv` provides historical monthly changes for the seasonal baseline.
- **The current dashboard forecast** is generated by `generate_live_xgboost_forecast.py` (also the default action of `forcast_reservoirs.py`) and saved in `reservoir_forecasts.csv`. The dashboard reads `dashboard_data.csv`, `metrics.csv`, and `reservoir_forecasts.csv`. The prior seasonal/damped-trend version is still available with `--model seasonal-baseline`.

The 15-day backtest measures forecast error, not the accuracy of satellite water-area extraction. The S2/S1 storage holdout measures satellite calibration, not the skill of a 15-day forecast. Keep these two score types separate.
