- `dashboard_data.csv`: loaded by `app.py`; active early-selected threshold S2 estimates and official history are assembled by `build_clear95_dashboard.py`.
- `metrics.csv`: loaded by `app.py`; active accuracy metrics are regenerated from later-holdout threshold-selected estimates by `build_clear95_dashboard.py`.
- `reservoir_forecasts.csv`: loaded by `app.py`; regenerated by `forcast_reservoirs.py`.
- `prepare_data.py`: model-comparison pipeline; its default outputs are candidate files and do not replace the active threshold-selected clear95 dashboard.
- `*_satellite_areas.csv`: generated by Earth Engine extraction scripts; do not overwrite originals without a successful rerun.
- `*_calibration_data.csv`: output of `merge_ground.py`; depends on a manually populated lookup and source observations.

The new scraper defaults to `cmwssb_scraped_observations.csv` and a companion `.report.json`. If `--base` is supplied, it normalizes dates, preserves populated cells, fills only blanks, and reports conflicts. No scraper run was performed as part of cleanup.
